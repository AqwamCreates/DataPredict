### 1. Reinforcement Learning Inside a Closed Game Platform: Combat NPCs Trained and Evaluated Entirely Within Roblox

- **Authors:** Micah Zhou and Weipeng Nie
- **Publication date:** October 6, 2026
- **Publication:** NHSJS
- **Paper:** [Read the research paper](https://nhsjs.com/2026/reinforcement-learning-inside-a-closed-game-platform-combat-npcs-trained-and-evaluated-entirely-within-roblox/)

**Relevance to DataPredict:** The paper cites DataPredict and its Self-Learning Sword-Fighting AIs example as prior work related to reinforcement learning implemented within Roblox. The authors subsequently investigate and quantitatively evaluate their own in-engine reinforcement-learning setup.
